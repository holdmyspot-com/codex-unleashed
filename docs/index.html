the developer experience

Your terminal has opinions.

You have deadlines. It has a 765 MiB rollout, a mystery spinner, and a strong preference for making you wait.

01 / latency

“Switching agents…”

You switch back to the main agent. The UI decides to reread a long session before acknowledging the keystroke.

02 / suspense

“Resuming session…”

No progress indicator. No bounded first page. Just a blank terminal and time to reconsider your career choices.

03 / drift

“Fixed upstream.”

Great. Now wait for the next release, then the next package build, then your organization’s upgrade window.

the approach

Small fixes. Clear receipts.

Every fix is public, scoped to an issue, and removable when upstream catches up. Codex Unleashed is intended to require an account and to bill usage by output tokens; pricing and allowances are not yet published.
