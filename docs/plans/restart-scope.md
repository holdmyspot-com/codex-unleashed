# Restart scope and reconciliation

## Design Goals

- Preserve the owner-selected migration outcomes, exclusions and acceptance evidence without treating old source inventories as implementation acceptance.
- Keep every old-main candidate and saved change available for an evidence-backed migration, omission or deferral decision.
- Preserve deferred billing, security, dependency and publication decisions without authorizing their implementation.

## Guidance

This file is the normative scope and reconciliation appendix to [plan.md](../../plan.md). Its D1–D13 decisions, supporting-change acceptance checks, deferred concerns and item-level inventory remain part of the restart. Use the fixed source and current checkpoint state in plan.md. The comparisons below retain their original source identities; references to an earlier stable baseline do not change the selected upstream-main pin or establish new acceptance on that pin. Revalidate a candidate when its consuming change requires it, subject to the owner's explicit reuse decision in plan.md.

The inventory records candidates and their individual decisions. It does not select every candidate for implementation, authorize publication or issue mutation, or convert a historical pending result into a new prerequisite. Update an affected row when its evidence establishes migration, an upstream equivalent, omission or deferral; preserve unaffected rows.

## Account and usage billing: decisions before implementation

The selected product model makes source visible, requires a HoldMySpot account for use, and bills output-token usage under pricing tiers published later. No fee is charged for early-access status. These facts replace old-main subscription and funding-based access policies.

Technical authentication, metering, billing enforcement, and the billable output-token definition are deferred to a later milestone. HoldMySpot usage charges are additional to users' model-provider charges; detailed customer documentation of that distinction is also deferred. Before implementing the later milestone, settle the token definition, identify existing HoldMySpot service/API contracts, and define supported failure/offline behavior. Keep pricing values unresolved until the owner publishes them; no charges or payment operations are authorized by this plan.
