# Support Policy

This repository does not provide general support for Codex.

## Maintainer Response Policy

The maintainer does not guarantee a response, fix, delivery date, or outcome for any request. Support and access do not depend on funding.

## Accounts and Usage Billing

Codex Unleashed is intended to require an account and to bill usage by output tokens. Pricing, allowances, and customer-facing billing documentation are not yet published; they will be released with the [Usage Terms](docs/terms.html) before they apply.

## Private Work

If you need private triage, custom work, support terms, SLA commitments, or other commercial terms, contact the maintainer for a separate written agreement.
